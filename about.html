About TaskMate

TaskMate is a simple to-do list that helps you remember what you need to do. It was built by our team as a group activity to learn Git and GitHub.

How to Use It

  1. Type a task in the box on the Home page.
  2. Click Add or press Enter.
  3. Tick the box when a task is done.
  4. Click Delete to remove a task.

Your tasks are saved in your own browser, so they stay even after you close the page.
